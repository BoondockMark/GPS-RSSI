Relative RSSI: 0 weaker → 100 stronger. Map outlines identify transceivers; dashed outlines mean closed squelch.

Points show mobile receive observations with qualified GPS. Unmeasured areas have no coverage rating. Fixed-site receive observations stay in charts and tables without being assigned a mobile location. RSSI is receiver-relative, not dBm or a voice-quality score. Different radio models and antennas need separate calibration.

Export and share this survey

Open measurements.gpkg or measurements.geojson in QGIS or ArcGIS, measurements.kml in Google Earth, or measurements.gpx in GPS software. GIS files contain qualified mobile receive points in WGS84 (longitude/latitude); they do not change with these view filters.

measurements.csv contains every selected sample, including TX, idle, and missing GPS. summary.json records the input paths, recording sessions, filters, and exclusions. recordings.sqlite retains all original input sessions, including sources excluded from this view, plus connection diagnostics. Use it in a later merge or survey without double-counting overlapping copies.

This HTML embeds its data, map library, and charts. Street tiles need Internet access; measured points and charts work offline. Share the folder or just this HTML using your preferred file-sharing service. Files contain precise location data and recording metadata.
