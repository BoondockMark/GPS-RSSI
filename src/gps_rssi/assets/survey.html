Synthetic training — not field measurements

Two replay runs and fictitious ground elevations let you test the controls without a radio or Internet. These values do not describe real coverage or terrain at the displayed locations. The presets enter a dummy station and antenna heights, select a reading, and run a local check. Presets change the analysis inputs; replay signal readings stay fixed and are not computed by a propagation model. You can then edit any input.

Path checks use adaptive ground samples, antenna heights and spherical Earth curvature. The amber envelope reserves 60% of the first Fresnel radius, using a near-horizontal, straight-path approximation. This is a planning guideline, not a coverage prediction. Buildings, trees and atmospheric refraction are omitted; narrow ridges between samples can be missed, including areas with finer data between queried points. Red rings mean a sampled line obstruction; amber means intrusion into the 60% Fresnel envelope; green means both checks pass at sampled locations and spacing targets were met. Gray means an unknown or sampling-limited result. Changing path settings clears prior results.

Export and share this survey

Open measurements.gpkg or measurements.geojson in QGIS or ArcGIS, measurements.kml in Google Earth, or measurements.gpx in GPS software. GIS files contain qualified mobile receive points in WGS84 (longitude/latitude); they do not change with these view filters.

measurements.csv contains every selected sample, including TX, idle, and missing GPS. summary.json records the input paths, recording sessions, filters, and exclusions. recordings.sqlite retains all original input sessions, including sources excluded from this view, plus connection diagnostics. Use it in a later merge or survey without double-counting overlapping copies.

This HTML embeds its data, map library, and charts. Choose a map background on the map. Missing or expired tiles need Internet access; measured points and charts work offline.
