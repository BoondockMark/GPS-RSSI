What the signal means

RSSI is a receiver-relative 0–100 reading. It is not dBm or a voice-quality score. A recent sample confirms telemetry; a running recorder alone does not confirm a radio connection.

Capture the receiving radio

Start this before sending the tone. This step does not transmit.

Stop ordinary recording on this radio first. Captures preserve received audio and timing information; incomplete captures stay marked as incomplete.

2

Prepare or transmit a tone

Previewing creates a file you can listen to locally. RF transmission requires a separate review.

Tone settings

Digital level does not establish RF modulation deviation.

A tone uses the radio’s current channel, power, and bandwidth. It lasts at most 10 seconds. Set these on the radio and start receiver capture first.

3

Measure the received signal

Analyze a finished capture to see signal quality over time.

SINAD measures the tone against noise and distortion. Higher is better. Results include Bluetooth and transmitter effects; they are not calibrated receiver sensitivity. Invalid audio stays unavailable, not zero. Imported WAV files are analyzed without capture timing metadata.
