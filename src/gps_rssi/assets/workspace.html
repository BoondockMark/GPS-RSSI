What the signal means

RSSI is a receiver-relative 0–100 reading. It is not dBm or a voice-quality score. A recent sample confirms telemetry; a running recorder alone does not confirm a radio connection.

PREPARE FOR THE HEIGHTS

Offline data

Keep maps and queried ground elevations on this computer, and carry them to another one.

Field mode

This prevents map-tile and elevation requests to external servers, including refresh requests. Expired saved entries remain usable. Reopen surveys and reload live views after changing this setting. Missing data stays unknown and is queued for later.

Prepare a regional map

Import a raster MBTiles map covering your driving area and zoom levels. Prepare it in a GIS tool from data you can use offline. It becomes the Offline map background in surveys and live views.

PNG/JPEG raster tiles, standard TMS rows, zooms 0–19. Vector PBF is unsupported. Import replaces the current regional map. File uploads are limited to 128 MB; use the offline command for larger files. Public map services here are used interactively; regional bulk download is not provided.

Use local terrain elevations

Unzip the La Habra Heights height-map package and import terrain_1m.tif (the complete rectangle) or terrain_1m_clipped.tif (the planning boundary). Terrain checks use this ground data first, including in Offline mode.

Outside the raster or at NoData cells, checks use saved or online USGS elevations according to Offline mode. GeoTIFFs must contain NAVD88 ground elevations in meters and use NAD83 / UTM zone 11N. Uploads support files up to 128 MB; use the offline terrain command for larger files. The 16-bit PNG and shaded preview cannot be imported. Reopen terrain checks after replacing or removing a raster.

Transfer prepared data

While connected, inspect the routes and radio paths you need. Then save a pack to retain their cached tiles and exact elevation points. Copy the ZIP to your field computer and import it there. Recordings travel separately using portable snapshots.

Saving pins cached data against automatic eviction. Packs include imported terrain, the regional map, cached basemaps, queried elevations with source metadata, and queued gaps. Counts describe saved entries, not complete geographic coverage. USGS point caches cannot supply a new location until it has been queried; map imagery does not supply elevation values.

Fill gaps after a drive

Reconnect, turn off Offline mode, and fill queued missing data. Open the same survey again and repeat the path inspection to use it. Adaptive paths may discover more points to fill. Gaps in an imported map need a new MBTiles file.

Each operation attempts up to 1,000 queued requests and stops after three failures. Progress and remaining counts appear in Activity. Filled entries are retained for offline use. Check the needed area and zoom levels in Offline mode before leaving.

1

Capture the receiving radio

Start this before sending the tone. This step does not transmit.

Stop ordinary recording on this radio first. Captures preserve received audio and timing information; incomplete captures stay marked as incomplete.

2

Prepare or transmit a tone

Previewing creates a file you can listen to locally. RF transmission requires a separate review.

Fixed starts at the next minute (:00, :20, :40); mobile starts 10 seconds later (:10, :30, :50). Uses the radio GPS clock when available, otherwise the computer clock. Start before driving; use Halt automatic mode to end the cycle.

Tone settings

Digital level does not establish RF modulation deviation.

A tone uses the radio’s current channel, power, and bandwidth. A single tone lasts at most 10 seconds; automatic mode repeats until halted. Set these on the radio and start receiver capture first.

3

Measure the received signal

Analyze a finished capture to see signal quality over time.

SINAD measures the tone against noise and distortion. Higher is better. Results include Bluetooth and transmitter effects; they are not calibrated receiver sensitivity. Invalid audio stays unavailable, not zero. Imported WAV files are analyzed without capture timing metadata.
